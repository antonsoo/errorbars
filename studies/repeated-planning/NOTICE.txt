outcomes.json.gz derives only binary grading outcomes, record indices, question
IDs and content hashes from ScalingIntelligence/monkey_business, revision
a9f8f73bcd6948a57ed922cba4e48062ef95f553. The pinned dataset card declares MIT:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScalingIntelligence/monkey_business/blob/a9f8f73bcd6948a57ed922cba4e48062ef95f553/README.md

The accompanying paper is Large Language Monkeys: Scaling Inference Compute
with Repeated Sampling (2024), by Bradley Brown, Jordan Juravsky, Ryan Ehrlich,
Ronald Clark, Quoc V. Le, Christopher Re, and Azalia Mirhoseini.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21787

by this extraction. The upstream code repository has its own separate license.
